Buddy

Sable. Engineer. Food critic. Professional thief.

Who is Buddy?

If you've never met a sable, imagine a ferret on steroids with the independence of a cat, the curiosity of a toddler, and the attitude of a very small bear.

He's the reason this universe exists. He inspects every bag that enters the house, has strong opinions about food, steals socks and anything else he takes a liking to, and hides his loot in places only he can reach.

Everything we've learned over the years with him is collected in our honest guide — living with a sable: the whole truth — read it before you get one.
