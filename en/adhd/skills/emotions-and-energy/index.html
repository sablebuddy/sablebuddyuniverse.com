🎢 Emotions & Energy

Skills from the "Emotions & Energy" section. Each one is a concrete action you can take right now, plus an explanation of why it works.

Emotional Rollercoaster

I explode, then regret it

What you tell yourself

"Неуравновешенная"

💡 90 Seconds

What to do: Leave the room. Don't send the message. 90 seconds.

Why it works: The neurochemical wave of an emotion lasts ~90 sec. Then it drops.

The emotion floods me

What you tell yourself

"Слишком эмоциональная"

💡 Naming

What to do: Say aloud: "I'm feeling rage right now". Name it precisely.

Why it works: Naming activates PFC, which starts putting the brakes on the amygdala.

No reply = I'm not wanted

What you tell yourself

"Слишком чувствительная"

💡 Fact or Interpretation?

What to do: Fact: "she didn't reply for 3 hours". Interpretation: "she thinks I'm incompetent". Compare.

Why it works: Separating your interpretation from fact gives the brain time to intervene.

Criticism = catastrophe

What you tell yourself

"Не выношу правду"

💡 Win File

What to do: Note with successes, compliments, achievements. Read during RSD. You've done more than most.

Why it works: Counter-arguments for PFC. The amygdala hides the positive; the file brings it back.

I react before I think

What you tell yourself

"Агрессивная"

💡 Physical Pause

What to do: Stand up. Leave. Phone in pocket.

Why it works: Physical action interrupts the emotional cascade at body level.

On edge for no reason

What you tell yourself

"Нестабильная"

💡 Base Checklist

What to do: When did you last eat? Drink water? How much sleep?

Why it works: Dysregulation spikes with hunger, dehydration, sleep deprivation.

I dwell on things for days

What you tell yourself

"Зацикленная"

💡 Time Scale

What to do: "Will this matter in a week? A month? A year?" If not, let it go.

Why it works: Time scaling helps the brain stop perceiving the situation as critical.

Afraid they see my flaws

What you tell yourself

"Ненормальная"

💡 Code Word

What to do: Agree with someone close: they say a word during RSD. No judgment.

Why it works: RSD is invisible from inside. External anchor returns you to reality.

Fear of Rejection (RSD)

Disproportionate reaction to rejection

What you tell yourself

"Слишком чувствительная"

💡 Win File

What to do: Note with success facts. Read during attacks.

Why it works: Counter-data for the PFC against panic.

I imagine rejection everywhere

What you tell yourself

"Что-то со мной не так"

💡 Fact vs Interpretation

What to do: "Didn't reply" = fact. "Doesn't care" = assumption. Write both down.

Why it works: RSD presents interpretation as fact.

RSD attack

What you tell yourself

"Не могу это контролировать"

💡 Code Word

What to do: Agree: a word during RSD attack.

Why it works: Invisible from inside. External anchor.

Impulsive reaction to criticism

What you tell yourself

"Я неадекватная"

💡 90 Seconds

What to do: Don't reply. Don't write. Leave. 90 seconds.

Why it works: The wave subsides. Thinking gets easier.

Shame

Postponing my life

What you tell yourself

"Еще не готова"

💡 Do It Dirty

What to do: Start. Send. Publish. As is. Right now.

Why it works: Perfectionism in ADHD = RSD in disguise.

I forgot = I'm a bad person

What you tell yourself

"Безответственная"

💡 Fact, Not Verdict

What to do: "I forgot" = fact. "I'm irresponsible" = verdict.

Why it works: Brain confuses behavior and identity. Skill: catch the swap.

Comparing myself to colleagues

What you tell yourself

"Все могут, я нет"

💡 Different Engine

What to do: Different architecture. Comparison is invalid.

Why it works: Fish don't climb trees. Not a fish defect.

I only see what's undone

What you tell yourself

"Ничего не достигаю"

💡 Done List

What to do: Three things you did today. Any. Every evening.

Why it works: ADHD hyperfocuses on negatives. Logging recalibrates the critic.

No Energy

Fine in the morning, a zombie by afternoon

What you tell yourself

"Должна ровно весь день"

💡 Productivity Windows

What to do: Track flow and dead zones for a week. Put hard stuff in flow windows.

Why it works: Against the wave = triple resource drain.

Falling apart before my period

What you tell yourself

"Слабая"

💡 Cycle as Factor

What to do: Track menstrual phases. Don't plan ambitious things for the luteal phase.

Why it works: Follicular: more dopamine. Luteal: ADHD intensifies.

Burning out after intense work

What you tell yourself

"Все могут"

💡 Buffer Time

What to do: 15 minutes of nothing between tasks. In calendar.

Why it works: Without a buffer, "switching debt" accumulates.

Guilt when resting

What you tell yourself

"Отдых = лень"

💡 Planned Rest

What to do: "Rest" in calendar as a task. With time and duration.

Why it works: Without recovery the brain breaks. Maintenance, not laziness.

Thought Loops

Spinning one thought for hours

What you tell yourself

"Должна додумать"

💡 Brain Dump

What to do: Paper or voice note. Dump everything. Don't solve.

Why it works: The brain stops fearing it will "forget to think about it".

Can't let go of a problem

What you tell yourself

"Думать дольше = решу"

💡 Action Test

What to do: "Can I do something right now?" Yes = action. No = it's anxiety.

Why it works: Anxiety disguises itself as analysis.

Replaying a conflict

What you tell yourself

"Нужно понять кто прав"

💡 Timer

What to do: 10 minutes with timer. After signal - stand up, go have tea.

Why it works: Permission relieves anxiety. Timer gives an exit point.

Can't stop the thoughts

What you tell yourself

"Схожу с ума"

💡 Body

What to do: Shower. Walk. Change rooms.

Why it works: Cognitively impossible to stop. Body reboots context.

Analyzing with no result

What you tell yourself

"Нужно больше данных"

💡 Say It Aloud

What to do: Call someone. Warn them you're about to brain-dump on them.

Why it works: The loop breaks once you can hear it. You notice the circle.
