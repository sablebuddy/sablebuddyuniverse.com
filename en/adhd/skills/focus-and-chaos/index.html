🎯 Focus & Chaos

Skills from the "Focus & Chaos" section. Each one is a concrete action you can take right now, plus an explanation of why it works.

On this page:
Getting DistractedForgetting EverythingEverything at OnceTime BlindnessMess Everywhere

Getting Distracted

Constantly distracted

What you tell yourself

"Дефицит внимания"

💡 Multi-thread

What to do: Background music + main task. Allow the parallel stream.

Why it works: Background stimulation maintains dopamine. Your architecture, not a defect.

Can't focus alone

What you tell yourself

"Зависимая от людей"

💡 Body Doubling

What to do: Focusmate, "study with me" stream, or call a friend and work silently.

Why it works: Others' presence creates a sense of accountability. Even if they're not watching. It works.

I forget about tasks

What you tell yourself

"Безответственная"

💡 Visual Triggers

What to do: Sticky notes, meds where you can see them. Don't hide anything important.

Why it works: Out of sight = fell into a parallel universe. Visibility = reminder.

On the phone 5 minutes later

What you tell yourself

"Нет силы воли"

💡 One In, One Out

What to do: Phone in another room. Close tabs. For 25 minutes.

Why it works: Removing temptations beats resisting them. Willpower is limited.

Can't get into flow

What you tell yourself

"Все легко начинают"

💡 Entry Ritual

What to do: Headphones + specific playlist + specific spot. Same every time.

Why it works: After 2-3 weeks brain enters focus automatically. Conditioned reflex.

Forgetting Everything

I forget thoughts within seconds

What you tell yourself

"Проблемы с памятью"

💡 Brain Dump

What to do: Voice note or write everything in your head. Right now.

Why it works: Unloading frees working memory and relieves "must not forget" anxiety.

I take notes in 5 places

What you tell yourself

"Неорганизованная"

💡 One System

What to do: ONE place for notes. Any. Delete all others.

Why it works: The ADHD brain gives up after the first failed search.

I nod and forget

What you tell yourself

"Мне все равно"

💡 Say It Back

What to do: "So you want me to..." Repeat the task in your own words.

Why it works: Converts from passive auditory memory to active processing.

Wrote it down, forgot why

What you tell yourself

"Идиотка"

💡 Context Notes

What to do: Not "Maria" but "Maria - March report, due Friday".

Why it works: A note without context is useless. Future you won't remember why.

I forget to take things

What you tell yourself

"Рассеянная"

💡 Visualization

What to do: Mentally see yourself picking up the item. 5 seconds.

Why it works: Visual scenes encode into a more durable type of memory.

Everything at Once

Everything feels equally important

What you tell yourself

"Не умею приоритизировать"

💡 What Burns First?

What to do: "What falls apart if I don't do it today?" That's first.

Why it works: The ADHD brain can't feel "important but not urgent". Loss threat triggers dopamine.

A 15-item to-do list

What you tell yourself

"Должна все успеть"

💡 Three Tasks

What to do: Cross out everything except three. Seriously.

Why it works: Short list doesn't overload working memory.

Don't know where to start

What you tell yourself

"Плохо планирую"

💡 Must / Should / Could

What to do: Must = disaster right now! Should = problem in a week. Could = oh well, the world won't end.

Why it works: Three buckets are simpler than an infinite hierarchy.

An hour planning instead of working

What you tell yourself

"Нужен план"

💡 5-Min Check-in

What to do: Open calendar. Pick three. Close. Start.

Why it works: Planning easily becomes procrastination in ADHD.

Time Blindness

I can't feel how much time passed

What you tell yourself

"Безответственная"

💡 Visual Timer

What to do: Time Timer on your desk where you can see it counting down.

Why it works: The ADHD brain sees time visually. Replaces the broken internal metronome.

Always late

What you tell yourself

"Плевать на людей"

💡 Reverse Planning

What to do: "Be there at 2pm". Subtract travel, prep, 15-min buffer. Set alarm for start.

Why it works: ADHD underestimates prep time. Reverse countdown + buffer compensates.

"5 minutes" = an hour

What you tell yourself

"Вру себе"

💡 Week of Measurements

What to do: Time your real duration for shower, prep, commute for a week. Write it down.

Why it works: Real data calibrates your internal estimates.

I can't estimate how long things take

What you tell yourself

"Плохо планирую"

💡 Rule of x2

What to do: Estimated the time? Multiply by two. Always.

Why it works: ADHD consistently underestimates. Multiplier compensates the blind spot.

A distant deadline doesn't exist

What you tell yourself

"Сделаю потом"

💡 Mini-Deadlines

What to do: Break into parts with deadlines every 1-2 days. With notifications.

Why it works: Near deadline = dopamine. Far deadline = parallel universe.

Mess Everywhere

Hunting for keys and phone

What you tell yourself

"Разгильдяйка"

💡 Home Base

What to do: Pick ONE spot for keys. Hook, bowl. ONLY there. Every time.

Why it works: The ADHD brain doesn't search - it runs on muscle memory. One spot = one route.

Desk buried under stuff

What you tell yourself

"Неряха"

💡 One Function

What to do: Desk = work. Remove everything else.

Why it works: Mixed functions kill the "place = action" association.

Tidied up, forgot where things are

What you tell yourself

"Ну и ладно"

💡 No Lids

What to do: Open baskets, clear containers.

Why it works: Must open = won't open. Invisible = nonexistent.

Clutter keeps piling up

What you tell yourself

"Когда-нибудь разберу"

💡 30 Seconds

What to do: Under 30 seconds to clean? Do it now. Don't think.

Why it works: "Later" in ADHD never comes. 30 seconds needs no motivation.
