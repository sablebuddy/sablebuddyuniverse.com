⚡ Launch & Braking

Skills from the "Launch & Braking" section. Each one is a concrete action you can take right now, plus an explanation of why it works.

Can't Start

Can't start, though I know I have to

What you tell yourself

"Я ленивая"

💡 2-Minute Rule

What to do: Say aloud: "just 2 minutes". Set a timer. Start the dumbest part.

Why it works: The brain doesn't resist short intervals. Inertia and dopamine pull you forward.

I know what to do, but I don't do it

What you tell yourself

"Нет мотивации"

💡 Anchor Habit

What to do: Pick an existing habit (coffee). Immediately after, do the new thing.

Why it works: Uses an existing neural pathway. No motivation generation needed.

I procrastinate, I want it perfect

What you tell yourself

"Я прокрастинатор"

💡 Ugly First Draft

What to do: Write the first sentence as badly as possible. Literally.

Why it works: Perfectionism blocks the start. Permission to be ugly removes the block.

Sitting frozen, can't move

What you tell yourself

"Что-то со мной не так"

💡 Countdown 5-4-3-2-1

What to do: Count aloud: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. At "1" stand up or open the file.

Why it works: Counting activates motor cortex and interrupts the procrastination loop.

The task is huge

What you tell yourself

"Не справлюсь"

💡 Micro-decomposition

What to do: Break into 5-10 minute steps. Do only the first one.

Why it works: Each completed chunk gives a microdose of dopamine.

Brain wants to, body won't

What you tell yourself

"Я сломана"

💡 Body First

What to do: Stand up. Walk. Pour water. Then sit down to work.

Why it works: Physical movement activates dopamine before cognitive work begins.

Hyperfocus - Can't Stop

Stuck scrolling for hours

What you tell yourself

"Моя суперсила"

💡 Soft Timers

What to do: Time Timer or gentle vibration every 45-60 minutes. No harsh alarms.

Why it works: Gentle signal doesn't cause stress. Brain resists exit less.

Can't stop

What you tell yourself

"Еще 5 минут..."

💡 Next Step

What to do: Before focusing, write: "after this I do X". Put the note visibly.

Why it works: Transition is easier when the decision is already made.

Disoriented after snapping out

What you tell yourself

"Где я, что случилось"

💡 Context Switch

What to do: Stand up. Leave the room. Change the lighting. Drink water.

Why it works: The brain ties the task to its environment. Changing the environment "closes the tab".

I don't hear people talking to me

What you tell yourself

"Плевать на людей"

💡 Interruption Partner

What to do: Agree with someone: hand on shoulder or code word. Not shouting from across the room.

Why it works: Physical signal penetrates where voice in hyperfocus doesn't.

'Must' Blocks Me

I want to, but "must" blocks me

What you tell yourself

"Саботирую себя"

💡 "I Choose"

What to do: Replace "I must do the report" with "I'll work with the data for 20 min". Or: "I choose to do this now so I can go to a cafe tonight".

Why it works: "Must" activates dorsolateral PFC in coercion mode + stress response. "I choose" activates ventromedial PFC - autonomy zone. Different neural route.

Simple task, body says no

What you tell yourself

"Ленивая"

💡 Element of Choice

What to do: "Which part do I do first?" Give yourself a choice.

Why it works: Command = freeze response. Choice switches brain from threat to agency.

"Should" = disgust

What you tell yourself

"Инфантильная"

💡 Word Swap

What to do: Delete "should". Replace with "decided", "choosing", "want to".

Why it works: These words activate different brain areas, bypassing resistance.

Frozen before anything boring

What you tell yourself

"Нормальные просто делают"

💡 Dopamine Pre-load

What to do: 5 minutes of something pleasant BEFORE the boring task.

Why it works: A brain with dopamine on board starts boring tasks more easily.
