Relationship Conflicts

"You never listen!"

What you tell yourself

"Мне все равно"

💡 Explain the Mechanism

What to do: "My brain can't hold verbal info during a task. Please text me."

Why it works: Instruction works better than apology.

I forget what was said

What you tell yourself

"Не ценю людей"

💡 Ask for Text

What to do: "Can you write it? I'll definitely remember that way."

Why it works: Text exists after the moment. Verbal doesn't in ADHD.

My partner feels forgotten

What you tell yourself

"Плохая партнерша"

💡 Check-in

What to do: "What do you need from me right now?" Once a day.

Why it works: Direct question replaces guessing.

Can't switch into a conversation

What you tell yourself

"Мне плевать"

💡 Honest Pause

What to do: "Can't listen well right now. Give me 10 minutes and I'm all yours."

Why it works: Honesty preserves relationships. Pretending to listen destroys them.

Already Got It, Still Talking

My brain got it, they keep talking

What you tell yourself

"Нетерпеливый, грубый"

💡 Mental Summary

What to do: Build a 3-bullet summary in your head while they're talking.

Why it works: Give the brain a job and it stops rebelling.

Want to shout "I got it!"

What you tell yourself

"Не умею слушать"

💡 Physical Patience Anchor

What to do: Clench fist, touch table, deep breath.

Why it works: Body "holds" you in the conversation.

They paused — urge to blurt it out

What you tell yourself

"Грубый"

💡 Turn It Into a Question

What to do: "So the key point is that...?" Don't start with "I already know"

Why it works: The speaker gets to wrap up via your question. Respect + tempo control.

Agony of waiting for them to finish

What you tell yourself

"Нетерпеливый"

💡 30-Second Rule

What to do: Listen 30 more seconds. Micro-task: "I'm just sitting for 30 seconds."

Why it works: People talk to organize their own thoughts. 30 seconds = relationship investment.

Interrupting & Blurting

The thought is burning to get out

What you tell yourself

"Невоспитанный"

💡 3-Second Rule

What to do: Speaker finishes - count: one, two, three. Then speak.

Why it works: Micro-gap between impulse and action. PFC gets time.

Afraid of losing the thought

What you tell yourself

"Не могу контролировать"

💡 Write the Thought

What to do: Jot it on paper instead of firing it into the conversation.

Why it works: Writing tells the brain "it's saved, you can keep listening." Reduces loss anxiety.

I blurt things out

What you tell yourself

"Агрессивный"

💡 Paraphrase First

What to do: "So you're saying that..." Then your response.

Why it works: Double benefit: speaker feels heard + you check if it's worth saying.

The urge to interrupt

What you tell yourself

"Не контролирую себя"

💡 "Stop" Anchor

What to do: Clench fist when you feel the urge to interrupt. Every time.

Why it works: After 2-3 weeks body associates the gesture with "wait". Conditioning.
