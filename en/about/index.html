Sable Buddy Universe

This project exists because of a small dark-brown sable named Buddy, who stole our hearts, our food and several household items. Around him grew a universe: a dramatic borzoi, a rabbit in management, two humans, and a habit of travelling to places with strange stories.

Sable Buddy Universe is an editorial project first: real stories, researched properly, told with the animals who were actually there.

Natalia

Natalia is responsible for storytelling, pet parenting and exploration. She researches and writes the stories. Big One films the videos, operates the drone and tests everything that ends up in the shop, ensuring logistical support to the entire operation.

Philosophy

  • ♡ Product recommendations come from real use and testing — nothing is listed because it pays well.
  • ♡ Stories are researched, not simply retold. If genetics or tide tables contradict the legend, we say so.
  • ♡ The animals are characters, not props. Their comfort outranks any shot.
